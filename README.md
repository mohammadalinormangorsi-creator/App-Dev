# Favorite Movie: Fast & Furious

**Fast & Furious** is an action film franchise centered around street racing, heists, and undercover operations led by *Dominic Toretto* and *Brian O'Conner*.

> "I don't have friends. I got family." — Dominic Toretto

---

## Why I Love the Franchise

- **High-Speed Action:** Custom muscle cars, import tuners, and intense street races.
- **Unbreakable Loyalty:** A tight-knit crew that treats each other like family.
- **Global Locations:** Set across Los Angeles, Tokyo, Rio de Janeiro, and London.

---

## Top 5 Favorite Movies in the Series

1. *The Fast and the Furious* (2001)
2. *The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift* (2006)
3. *Furious 7* (2015)
4. *F9: The Fast Saga* (2021)
5. *Fast X* (2023)

### Iconic Car Specs
